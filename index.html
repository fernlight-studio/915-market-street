      The Home

      Built for the way you live, inside and out

      Built in 2024 by LNL Builds, this Northwest Contemporary home sits in Kirkland's sought-after West of Market neighborhood, a short walk from downtown and the waterfront parks. Three levels offer 4,378 sq ft with 5 bedrooms and 5.5 baths.

      The great room opens through wide sliding glass doors to an outdoor living deck with its own gas fireplace, so entertaining flows easily between inside and out. Upstairs, the covered upper deck looks west to Lake Washington, the Seattle skyline and the Olympic Mountains. The kitchen is built around Gaggenau and Miele appliances, and the lower level adds a media room, a wine room and a gym.
